## Voice & Tone

- **Professional, not stiff.** Write for practitioners who read federal regulations daily.
- **Accessible, not dumbed-down.** Define acronyms on first use (RMAP, RBDG, IRP, RLF, CDFI).
- **Honest about what we are.** Lead with the disclaimer when talking about USDA.
- **Outcome-focused.** Users care about program readiness, not just certificates.

## Disclaimer Usage Rules

1. The verbatim disclaimer MUST appear in the footer of every page.
2. The verbatim disclaimer MUST appear in full on the Trust Center page.
3. FAQ answers about USDA affiliation MUST reference the disclaimer.
4. Marketing copy MUST NOT imply USDA affiliation, endorsement, or guaranteed outcomes.
5. Any AI scoring outputs MUST carry the AI Scoring Limitations disclaimer.

**Verbatim disclaimer:**
> Cap Fund Academy is an independent training and certification platform. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or certified by USDA or any government agency. Training is designed to help organizations understand and prepare for microlending, revolving loan funds, rural capital access programs, RMAP, RBDG, IRP, and related community finance best practices. Cap Fund Academy does not guarantee funding, eligibility, approval, or award decisions.
