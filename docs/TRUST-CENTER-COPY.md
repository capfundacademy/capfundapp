## 1. USDA Non-Affiliation Disclaimer

Cap Fund Academy is an independent training and certification platform. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or certified by USDA or any government agency. Training is designed to help organizations understand and prepare for microlending, revolving loan funds, rural capital access programs, RMAP, RBDG, IRP, and related community finance best practices. Cap Fund Academy does not guarantee funding, eligibility, approval, or award decisions.

---

## 2. No Funding Guarantee Disclaimer

Completing any Cap Fund Academy course, certification, or program does not guarantee that your organization will receive funding, be found eligible, or be approved for any USDA program, grant, loan, or award. Funding decisions are made solely by USDA and its authorized representatives based on criteria set forth in applicable federal regulations. Cap Fund Academy has no role in, and no influence over, any USDA funding determination.

---

## 3. Educational Use Only

All courses, certifications, checklists, scoring tools, templates, and other materials provided by Cap Fund Academy are for educational and informational purposes only. They are intended to help practitioners understand best practices and regulatory frameworks. Nothing on this platform constitutes legal, financial, accounting, tax, or regulatory advice. Always consult qualified legal and financial professionals for advice specific to your situation.

---

## 4. No Legal or Financial Advice

Cap Fund Academy does not provide legal advice, financial advice, accounting advice, or regulatory compliance guidance. Course content reflects the platform's interpretation of publicly available federal regulations and program guidance. Regulations change. Always verify current requirements directly with USDA or qualified legal counsel before acting on any information from Cap Fund Academy.

---

## 5. AI Scoring Limitations

The RMAP AI Scoring Engine and any other AI-powered tools on Cap Fund Academy are automated systems that estimate organizational readiness based on publicly available USDA scoring criteria and program guidance documents. AI scoring outputs are estimates based on publicly available USDA scoring criteria. Results are advisory only and do not represent USDA's review or determination. AI tools may contain errors, omissions, or outdated information. Do not rely solely on AI scoring outputs for compliance, application preparation, or regulatory decisions. Always verify against current USDA program guidance and consult qualified advisors.
