RMAP Eligibility Checklist

Cap Fund Academy · Certificate 3: USDA RMAP Eligibility, Application & Scoring · capfundacademy.com

Use this checklist to confirm your organization meets the minimum eligibility requirements for the USDA Rural Microentrepreneur Assistance Program (RMAP) before assembling your application. All items in Section A must be met. Items in Sections B–D determine your applicant pathway.

Section A — Universal Eligibility Requirements (All Applicants)

RequirementRegulatory CitationNotes / Evidence
Applicant is a private, nonprofit organization OR a public agency OR an Indian tribe (as defined in 25 U.S.C. 4103)7 CFR 4280.303
Applicant is legally organized and in good standing in the state of incorporation7 CFR 4280.303
Applicant has a primary mission of economic development or microenterprise development7 CFR 4280.303
Applicant's service area is rural (USDA-designated rural area)7 CFR 4280.303
Applicant has active SAM.gov registration with current UEI2 CFR 25
Applicant has no delinquent federal debt or outstanding federal judgments7 CFR 4280.303
Applicant is not debarred or suspended from federal programs (check SAM.gov Exclusions)2 CFR 180
Applicant will comply with civil rights requirements (ECOA, Title VI, ADA, Section 504)7 CFR 4280.303

Check the pathway that applies. Your pathway determines which scoring criteria and documentation requirements apply.
