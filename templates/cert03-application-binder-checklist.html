RMAP Application Binder Checklist

Cap Fund Academy · Certificate 3: USDA RMAP Eligibility, Application & Scoring · capfundacademy.com

Use this checklist to assemble a complete RMAP application binder. Check each item as present and properly labeled. Submit to your USDA Rural Development State Office per their instructions.

Tab 1 — Application Forms and Certifications

✓DocumentNotes / Location
Completed USDA RD application form (signed by authorized representative)
Form AD-1047: Certification Regarding Debarment and Suspension
Lobbying certification (SF-LLL if lobbying funds were used)
Drug-free workplace certification
SAM.gov active registration printout with current UEI
Civil rights compliance certifications

Tab 2 — Organizational Documents

✓DocumentNotes / Location
Articles of incorporation and bylaws (current, certified copy)
IRS 501(c)(3) determination letter or tribal authority documentation
Current board of directors list with bios (including relevant experience)
Current organizational chart
Key staff résumés (loan officer, program director, executive director)
Succession plan or key person controls documentation

Tab 3 — Financial Documents

✓DocumentNotes / Location
Audited or reviewed financial statements — most recent 2-3 fiscal years
Current fiscal year budget
3-year RLF financial pro forma (loan volume, revenue, expenses)
RMRF and LLRF account statements (if existing program)

Tab 4 — Program Documents

✓DocumentNotes / Location
Written loan fund policy (covers all required elements per 7 CFR 4280.315)
Microloan program description and delivery plan
Technical Assistance & Training (TA&T) plan
3-year microloan portfolio history (experienced applicants)
Work plan (less-experienced applicants)

Tab 5 — Evidence and Community Support

✓DocumentNotes / Location
Rural service area documentation (USDA ERS RUCA codes, Census data)
Community needs documentation (poverty rate, unemployment, capital access gap)
Letters of support / commitment (minimum 3, target 5-7)
Partnership MOUs or agreements
Job creation/retention data (if existing program)
Borrower demographic data summary

Tab 6 — Environmental and Compliance

✓DocumentNotes / Location
Environmental screening worksheet (program-level, not loan-level)
Civil rights compliance documentation (LEP plan, complaint procedure)
Application complete — all tabs assembled
Reviewed by (person who did NOT write the application)
Submission date / method
State Office contact / confirmation received
⚠ Requirements may vary by state office. Always confirm with your USDA Rural Development State Office before submitting. Cap Fund Academy is not affiliated with USDA.
