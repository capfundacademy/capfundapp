Microloan Credit Policy Outline

Cap Fund Academy · Certificate 8: Underwriting, Credit Policy & Portfolio Risk · capfundacademy.com

A complete written loan policy is required by USDA for RMAP-approved microlenders. This outline ensures your policy covers all required sections. Draft each section in your policy document using this as the table of contents and checklist.

✓Policy SectionRequired ElementsStatus
1. Purpose and ScopeMission alignment, federal program authority (7 CFR 4280), geographic service area
2. Loan AuthorityLoan committee composition, quorum, approval thresholds, board escalation triggers
3. Eligible BorrowersEntity types, rural requirement, 10 FTE or fewer, credit elsewhere requirement, ineligible borrower categories
4. Eligible Loan PurposesAllowed uses (working capital, equipment, inventory, etc.); RMAP ineligible uses per 7 CFR 4280.326
5. Loan TermsMinimum/maximum amounts, maximum term (RMAP: 10 years), interest rate range, origination fee, prepayment policy
6. Underwriting StandardsCredit score policy, global debt service ratio, cash flow analysis method, collateral requirements, personal guarantee
7. Application ProcessRequired documents, environmental screening, rural verification, credit report, credit elsewhere
8. Loan Committee ProcessMeeting frequency, decision documentation, adverse action notice requirements, ECOA compliance
9. Loan ClosingRequired documents, security interest (UCC, mortgage), insurance, disbursement controls
10. Loan ServicingPayment processing, annual review, delinquency monitoring, contact cadence
11. Collections and WorkoutsDelinquency escalation procedures, workout authority, default declaration, charge-off authority
12. RMRF/LLRF ManagementAccount segregation, LLRF minimum (5%), program income allocation, prohibited uses
13. Conflict of InterestDefinition of insider, disclosure requirements, recusal procedure, insider transaction prohibition or approval process
14. Record RetentionLoan file requirements, retention period (3 years post-final report per 2 CFR 200.334), access controls
15. Policy ReviewAnnual review requirement, board approval of amendments, USDA notification of material changes
