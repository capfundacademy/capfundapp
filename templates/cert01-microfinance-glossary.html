Microfinance & RLF Glossary

Cap Fund Academy · Certificate 1: Microfinance Foundations · capfundacademy.com
TermDefinition
MDOMicroenterprise Development Organization — a nonprofit or public entity whose primary mission includes providing microloan capital and technical assistance to microenterprises.
MicroloanUnder RMAP: a loan of up to $50,000 to a rural microenterprise. Generally in the field: a loan smaller than what conventional lenders offer, typically under $50,000.
MicroenterpriseUnder RMAP (7 CFR 4280.303): a sole proprietorship, partnership, or corporation with 10 or fewer full-time equivalent employees and principal business operations in a rural area.
RMRFRural Microentrepreneur Revolving Fund — the restricted account holding RMAP grant funds and microloan repayments, used exclusively for making new RMAP-eligible microloans.
LLRFLoan Loss Reserve Fund — a separate restricted account holding at least 5% of outstanding RMAP microloan principal, used to cover losses on defaulted microloans.
RLFRevolving Loan Fund — a pool of capital that is loaned to borrowers, repaid, and re-loaned, "revolving" the same capital repeatedly.
RMAPRural Microentrepreneur Assistance Program — a USDA Rural Development program providing loans to MDOs to capitalize microenterprise revolving loan funds, plus grants for technical assistance.
RBDGRural Business Development Grant — a USDA Rural Development grant program for rural public entities and nonprofits to establish RLFs, business incubators, and other rural business development activities.
IRPIntermediary Relending Program — a USDA Rural Development direct loan program at 1% interest to eligible intermediaries, who relend the funds to rural businesses at market rates.
TA&TTechnical Assistance and Training — services provided by MDOs to help microentrepreneurs develop business skills, create plans, manage finances, and prepare for loan applications.
Credit ElsewhereA finding that a loan applicant cannot obtain credit from conventional sources at reasonable rates and terms — required documentation for RMAP microloan eligibility.
PARPortfolio at Risk — the percentage of the outstanding loan portfolio with payments past due by a specified number of days (PAR 30, PAR 60, PAR 90).
CDFICommunity Development Financial Institution — a Treasury-certified organization whose primary mission is to provide financial services to underserved communities.
UEIUnique Entity Identifier — the 12-character alphanumeric identifier assigned by SAM.gov, replacing the DUNS number for federal grant and contract recipients.
SAM.govSystem for Award Management — the federal government database where all entities doing business with the government must register and maintain an active record.
7 CFR 4280Title 7 of the Code of Federal Regulations, Part 4280 — the regulation governing USDA Business and Industry loan guarantees, RMAP, RBDG, and related rural business programs.
2 CFR 200Title 2 of the Code of Federal Regulations, Part 200 — the Uniform Guidance governing administration of all federal grants and cooperative agreements.
SEFASchedule of Expenditures of Federal Awards — the audited schedule listing all federal awards expended during a fiscal year, required for single audits under 2 CFR 200 Subpart F.
SF-270Standard Form 270 — Request for Advance or Reimbursement, used to draw down federal grant funds.
OGCOffice of General Counsel — USDA's legal office that reviews and clears loan closing documents for RMAP awards.
ECOAEqual Credit Opportunity Act — federal law prohibiting discrimination in credit transactions based on race, color, religion, national origin, sex, marital status, or age.
UCCUniform Commercial Code — the body of commercial law governing secured transactions; a UCC-1 financing statement perfects a lender's security interest in collateral.
Charge-offThe accounting write-off of a loan balance deemed uncollectible; the loan is removed from active receivables and recorded as a loss.
WorkoutA negotiated modification of loan terms (payment deferral, rate reduction, term extension) for a borrower experiencing temporary difficulty, as an alternative to default.
