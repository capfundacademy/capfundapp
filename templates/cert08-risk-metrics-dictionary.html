Portfolio Risk Metrics Dictionary

Cap Fund Academy · Certificate 8: Underwriting, Credit Policy & Portfolio Risk · capfundacademy.com
MetricFormulaTarget / BenchmarkInterpretation
PAR 30Outstanding balance of loans 30+ days past due ÷ Total outstanding portfolio × 100<10%Primary early-warning indicator. Above 10% requires investigation and intervention.
PAR 60Outstanding balance 60+ days past due ÷ Total outstanding × 100<5%Moderate delinquency; most loans at this stage require active workout discussions.
PAR 90Outstanding balance 90+ days past due ÷ Total outstanding × 100<3%Severe delinquency; default declaration likely unless significant change occurs.
Default RateLoans declared in default ÷ Total loans originated (same period) × 100Program-specificMeasures underwriting quality over time. Rising rates signal policy or market issues.
Charge-Off RateLoan balances written off ÷ Average outstanding portfolio × 100<5% for microloansActual realized losses. Compare to LLRF reserve adequacy.
Recovery RateCash recovered on charged-off loans ÷ Total charged-off balances × 100Program-specificHigher is better. Drives decisions on pursuing collections on charged-off accounts.
DSCRNet operating income ÷ Total debt service payments≥1.20x (policy minimum)Measures repayment capacity. Below 1.0x means insufficient cash flow to cover obligations.
LTV RatioLoan amount ÷ Collateral value × 100Policy-specific (e.g., ≤80%)Lower LTV = lower lender risk if collateral must be liquidated.
Capital UtilizationOutstanding loan balance ÷ Total available RLF capital × 10075-90%Below 75% = under-deployed capital; above 95% = may not have capital for new loans.
Allowance for Loan Losses (ALL)Estimated credit loss reserve on balance sheet≥historical loss rate × portfolioAccounting estimate of expected losses. Must be adequately funded to present accurate financials.
LLRF CoverageLLRF balance ÷ Outstanding RMAP microloan principal × 100≥5% (USDA requirement)RMAP-specific reserve requirement. Below 5% requires immediate replenishment from program income.
Portfolio ConcentrationLoans in any single industry or borrower ÷ Total portfolio × 100<25% per sectorHigh concentration increases correlated default risk if that sector experiences a downturn.
