Article I — Purpose

The Loan Committee is established to review and approve or deny all loan applications submitted to the organization's revolving loan fund(s), in accordance with the organization's written loan fund policy and applicable federal program requirements.

Article III — Authority

The Loan Committee has full authority to approve or deny loan applications up to $. Loans above this amount require full Board approval. The Loan Committee may not approve loans to insiders (officers, directors, board members, or their immediate family members) without prior Board approval and, where required, USDA notification.

Article IV — Meeting Procedures

Frequency: The Loan Committee meets at least per month, or as needed when loan applications are pending.

Notice: Meeting notice provided at least days in advance.

Documentation: Written minutes are prepared within business days of each meeting and include: date, attendees, applications reviewed, votes, conditions, and any dissenting opinions.

Article V — Conflict of Interest

Any committee member with a personal, financial, or familial interest in a loan application must: (1) disclose the conflict in writing before the meeting, (2) recuse from all discussion and voting on that application, and (3) leave the room during deliberation. Recusals are documented in the minutes.

Article VI — Loan Decision Standards

All loan decisions are based on the written underwriting criteria in the organization's Loan Fund Policy. Decisions must be documented with specific reasons for approval (including any conditions) or denial. Denial reasons are used to draft adverse action notices within 30 days as required by ECOA.

Article VII — Amendment

This charter may be amended by a majority vote of the full Board of Directors. No amendment may reduce the quorum requirement below a majority of voting members.
