Civil Rights Compliance Checklist for USDA-Funded RLFs

Cap Fund Academy · Certificate 10: Federal Compliance · capfundacademy.com

Use this checklist to assess civil rights compliance readiness. Review annually and before any USDA site visit. All items are required for RMAP and RBDG recipients.

ECOA — Equal Credit Opportunity Act (15 U.S.C. § 1691)

✓RequirementEvidence / Document Location
Written underwriting criteria applied consistently to all applicants
Adverse action notices issued in writing with specific reasons within 30 days of decision
Adverse action log maintained with date, reason, and method of delivery for each denied application
Loan officers do not ask about race, religion, national origin, or sex during underwriting
Annual staff training on ECOA requirements documented

Title VI — Civil Rights Act of 1964 (42 U.S.C. § 2000d)

✓RequirementEvidence / Document Location
All program services (outreach, TA, lending) are equally accessible to all racial/color/national origin groups in the service area
Marketing materials are available in languages spoken by significant LEP populations in the service area
Demographic data collected on all loan applicants (voluntary self-identification, separate from credit file)
Limited English Proficiency (LEP) Plan is written, current (updated within 3 years), and posted
LEP Plan identifies languages in service area, available assistance, and staff training requirements

ADA and Section 504

✓RequirementEvidence / Document Location
Physical office space is accessible (ramp, accessible restroom, parking)
Program communications available in accessible formats upon request
Online platforms meet WCAG 2.1 AA accessibility standards
Section 504 Coordinator designated (required if 15+ employees) and posted
Written grievance procedure for Section 504 complaints exists and is distributed

Complaint Procedure

✓RequirementEvidence / Document Location
Written civil rights complaint procedure is posted in the office and on the website
Complaint procedure available in service area languages
Complaint log maintained (even if no complaints received — empty log proves system exists)
Complaints logged include: date received, nature, action taken, and resolution
Complaint procedure preserves the complainant's right to file with USDA Office of Civil Rights

Training and Documentation

✓RequirementDate / Evidence
Annual civil rights training for all staff who interact with loan applicants or program participants
Training records retained (attendees, date, topics covered)
New staff civil rights orientation within 30 days of hire
