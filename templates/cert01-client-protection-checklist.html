Client Protection Checklist for Microlenders

Cap Fund Academy · Certificate 1: Microfinance Foundations · capfundacademy.com

Review this checklist annually and before launching new loan products. Based on the Smart Campaign Client Protection Principles for microfinance institutions.

1 — Appropriate Product Design and Delivery

✓StandardEvidence / Notes
Loan products designed to meet borrower needs, not maximize fee income
Loan sizes appropriate to borrower repayment capacity (not maximum allowed)
Repayment schedules aligned with borrower cash flow cycles
No hidden fees; all costs disclosed upfront in writing

2 — Prevention of Over-Indebtedness

✓StandardEvidence / Notes
Repayment capacity assessed before every loan (cash flow analysis)
Credit bureau check or equivalent performed on all applicants
Total debt obligations reviewed (not just this loan)
Credit elsewhere requirement documented for RMAP loans

3 — Transparency

✓StandardEvidence / Notes
Effective annual interest rate (APR) disclosed in writing before signing
All fees, penalties, and charges listed in the loan agreement
Borrower given time to read and ask questions before signing
Loan terms explained verbally in borrower's preferred language

4 — Responsible Pricing

✓StandardEvidence / Notes
Interest rates are reasonable relative to cost of funds and mission
No predatory fees (origination fees, prepayment penalties reviewed annually)
Pricing reviewed against peer microlenders annually

5 — Fair and Respectful Treatment

✓StandardEvidence / Notes
Staff trained on respectful collections — no harassment or intimidation
Written collections policy prohibits abusive practices
Non-discrimination policy covers all protected classes

6 — Privacy of Client Data

✓StandardEvidence / Notes
Privacy policy exists and is provided to borrowers
Borrower data not shared without written consent (except as legally required)
Demographic data stored separately from credit file

7 — Mechanisms for Complaint Resolution

✓StandardEvidence / Notes
Written complaint procedure exists and is communicated to borrowers
Complaint log maintained; complaints reviewed quarterly
Complaint trends reviewed to identify systemic issues
