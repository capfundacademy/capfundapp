Microenterprise Borrower Readiness Scorecard

Cap Fund Academy · Certificate 1: Microfinance Foundations · capfundacademy.com

Use this scorecard to assess whether a potential borrower is ready for a microloan. Score each section 0-5. A total score of 30+ indicates readiness to proceed to full application. Under 20 suggests TA focus before loan consideration.
